The gate's ledger. Wave 1 produced 72 raw findings across twelve lenses; grouped by site they are the rows below. Two lenses reaching the same site is one row naming both: agreement raises confidence, never the count. Detail, failure sequences and the hunt items found sound are in the twelve `docs/reports/gate2-*-review.md` files, which this table does not replace.

The exit condition of `docs/reviews/gate-2-plan.md` §6 is checked against this file.

**Wave 3 ran on 2026-08-30**, six implementers in one parallel wave, none of them the author of the
code they fixed. Rows closed since carry their state and what proves it. Two rows are recorded as
**not a defect** with an argument rather than a fix, and several as **not fixed** with a reason —
both are legitimate outcomes under WORKFLOW 4a, which asks for every finding to be fixed or
explicitly ruled on, and the reasons are in the six `gate2-fix-*.md` reports.

**Only five rows carry a state here, and that is deliberate.** The rest stay `open` until Wave 4's
closure audit sets them, because the plan's own rule is that a row's state comes from re-running its
hunt item against the tree and never from the fix's description of itself. Marking fifty-four rows
closed on their authors' word would turn this ledger into a summary of intentions. The five that are
already stated are the ones with a measurement outside the fixer's own account: the two blockers,
each with a check that fails without it; the mitigation, with an armed-and-disarmed run on the
owner's display; and the two CI rows, whose closure is visible in the workflow file.
